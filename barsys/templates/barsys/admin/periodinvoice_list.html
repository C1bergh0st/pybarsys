Explanation

An invoice by period sums up the purchases of each user over a period. It either ends now or at a date and time you choose, and starts where the previous invoice before it ends (the first one covers all purchases before it). Free item purchases are not included.
The sums are fixed when the invoice is created. Unlike the per-user invoices, creating one does not bill any purchases and does not change account balances.
Periods never overlap. Deleting an invoice leaves a gap, which an invoice until the deleted one's end covers again.
